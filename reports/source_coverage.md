**How to read this.** These are stored, delayed quotes used for research. Nothing here is a live or executable price.

- **Observed fresh** means a stored quote carries a timestamp at most 24h old. What that timestamp is depends on the source (see *Age basis* in the matrix). For ESPN it is only when Doyen first saw the price, so a small age shows the price was recently *observed*, not that the bookmaker recently *repriced* it.
- **Bookmaker-timed fresh** counts only quotes whose timestamp is the bookmaker's own update time (at most 24h old).
- **Operators** are a range. *Lower* counts only bookmakers whose owner or pricing platform is recorded in the map in `sbi/sources/books.py`. *Upper* also treats every bookmaker of unknown ownership as a separate operator. Unknown ownership is never assumed independent, so anything above the lower bound is provisional.
